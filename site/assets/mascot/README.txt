Mascot clips
============

idle.webm   idle breathing loop
look.webm   glancing around (played every ~18s)
tap.webm    reaction to a click

Source: dsh-pet 0.3.8 by PC2005-cloud — https://github.com/PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet
Licence: MIT (see the package LICENSE). The artwork was generated with AI
by that project's author; it is used here unmodified as decorative content
for a coursework prototype and is credited in the design report (section 5.2).

These files are plain VP9-with-alpha WebM, served locally. Nothing is fetched
from the network and no model is called when they play.
